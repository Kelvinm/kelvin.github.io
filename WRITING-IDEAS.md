# Writing Ideas

Observations and takes that could become short posts (500-800 words). Don't overthink these—clear thinking > polish.

---

## AI Adoption in Financial Services

- **"Why most AI pilots fail in regulated environments"**  
  The gap between demo and production. Compliance, data governance, model risk management. Why "move fast" doesn't work here.

- **"The LLM adoption curve at financial firms"**  
  What I'm seeing: Copilot for code is easy, customer-facing AI is hard, internal research automation is the sweet spot.

- **"AI won't replace your analysts—but it will change what they do"**  
  The 25-30 hours/week savings on research. What happens when you give that time back.

- **"Document processing is the unsexy AI use case that actually works"**  
  SEC filings, earnings calls, PDFs to structured data. Why this beats chatbots for ROI.

## Technology Leadership

- **"What I look for when inheriting a technology team"**  
  First 90 days. The signals that tell you what's really going on.

- **"The CTO's job isn't to be the best engineer"**  
  Translation layer between business and technology. When to go deep, when to stay out.

- **"Build vs buy decisions in 2025"**  
  The calculus has changed. Vendor risk, integration debt, when "buy" becomes "build anyway."

- **"Why your data platform initiative is stalling"**  
  Governance theater, lack of executive sponsorship, and the "build it and they will come" fallacy.

## Format Notes

- Lead with the point, not the context
- One idea per post
- Okay to be opinionated—that's the point
- Don't need to cite sources for observations from experience
- End with a question or implication, not a summary
